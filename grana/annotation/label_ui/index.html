How to label4 steps
  1. Enter your name, then pick an episode.
  2. Pick a subtask brush above the Subtask timeline and drag to paint it until the clip is covered; click C / F at a subtask's end to set its outcome.
  3. Pick Suboptimal or Regressed above the Annotation timeline and drag over the moments that went wrong.
  4. Check the Converted progress curve, tick the review box, then Submit.
